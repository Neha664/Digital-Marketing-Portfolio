# NOVA Skin — 7 Days to Better Skin

## Campaign Overview

**Brand:** NOVA Skin  
**Campaign:** 7 Days to Better Skin  
**Campaign Type:** AI-assisted digital marketing campaign  
**Campaign Period:** July 2026

NOVA Skin is a fictional skincare brand created for this portfolio project.

The campaign was designed around a simple idea: encourage customers to build a consistent skincare routine over seven days while using data to understand which audiences and channels respond most effectively.

## Campaign Objective

The campaign objectives were:

- Increase awareness among younger skincare consumers.
- Generate website traffic and product interest.
- Identify high-performing digital channels.
- Compare audience response across content formats.
- Generate conversions while monitoring advertising spend.
- Use campaign data to identify opportunities for future optimization.

## Target Audiences

### Gen Z
Interested in short-form, visually engaging content and affordable products.

### Young Professionals
Interested in convenient skincare routines and practical recommendations.

### Skincare Seekers
Actively searching for solutions to specific skincare needs.

### Existing Customers
Already familiar with the brand and more likely to respond to personalized offers.

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Awareness and engagement |
| Google Ads | High-intent traffic |
| Email | Retention and conversion |

## AI-Assisted Approach

AI tools were considered for:

- Generating initial content ideas.
- Creating alternative ad-copy variations.
- Brainstorming audience-specific messaging.
- Producing campaign themes.
- Suggesting A/B testing ideas.

Final campaign decisions were based on the campaign dataset and marketing analysis rather than AI-generated suggestions alone.

## Key KPIs

The campaign tracks:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversions
- Conversion rate
- Spend
- Revenue
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

## Optimization Questions

The analysis focuses on:

1. Which channel generated the strongest conversion performance?
2. Which audience segments showed stronger response?
3. Which content formats generated higher engagement?
4. Which campaigns generated stronger revenue relative to spend?
5. Where could future campaign budget be optimized?

## Portfolio Note

This project uses synthetic data created specifically for portfolio analysis. It demonstrates the process of campaign planning, marketing analytics, KPI evaluation and data-driven optimization.

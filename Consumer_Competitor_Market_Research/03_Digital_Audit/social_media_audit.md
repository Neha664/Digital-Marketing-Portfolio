# Social Media Audit Framework

## What should be monitored

| Content behavior | Marketing purpose |
|---|---|
| Short educational videos | Reach + discovery |
| Ingredient explainers | Saves + trust |
| Creator demonstrations | Social proof |
| Before/after stories | Consideration |
| Polls/quizzes | First-party insight |
| Product launches | Conversion |
| Routine guides | Decision support |
| UGC/community | Trust + retention |

## Recommended audit scorecard

For a future live audit, record for each competitor:
- posting frequency
- content format mix
- educational vs promotional ratio
- recurring topics
- creator/UGC usage
- CTA patterns
- comment themes
- engagement rate

### Important

Do not invent engagement metrics. They should be collected from the relevant platform at the time of analysis.

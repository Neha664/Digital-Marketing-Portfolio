# Content Audit

## Category content territories

### Territory A — Ingredient Education
High strategic relevance because ingredient literacy is increasing.

### Territory B — Skin Concern
Acne, pigmentation, dryness, texture and sun protection.

### Territory C — Product Benefits
Functional reasons to buy.

### Territory D — Lifestyle/Experience
Texture, visual identity, self-care and community.

### Territory E — Routine Education
Sequencing, frequency, compatibility and expectation.

## Audit conclusion

The first four territories are highly visible in the category. The fifth can become a stronger **organizing principle** for the proposed marketing strategy.

The opportunity is not “competitors do not talk about routines.” The opportunity is to make **routine decision support the central content architecture**.

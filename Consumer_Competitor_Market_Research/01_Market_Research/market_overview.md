# Market Overview — Indian Skincare

## Executive Brief

## Market Forces

### 1. Ingredient literacy
Consumers increasingly encounter ingredient names and efficacy claims.

### 2. Digital discovery
Beauty discovery increasingly happens online, with social platforms influencing exploration. citeturn0search11

### 3. Younger consumer influence
Mintel identifies Gen Z and younger Millennials as important facial-skincare users. citeturn0search5

### 4. Simplicity
Consumers may want targeted improvements without unnecessarily complicated routines.

### 5. Trust
Transparency, reviews and evidence matter in a crowded category.

## Marketing implication

A brand can compete not only on product efficacy but on **decision clarity**: making the path from concern to routine easier.

# Target Consumer Definition

## Primary Strategic Audience

**Digitally active skincare shoppers who are informed enough to research products but still experience decision friction when building routines.**

### Need states

**Learn:** “What does this ingredient do?”

**Choose:** “Which product fits my concern?”

**Sequence:** “What should I use first?”

**Validate:** “Is this claim credible?”

**Continue:** “What should I do consistently?”

## Marketing implication

The target is defined by **decision behavior and need state**, not by an unsupported demographic stereotype.

This allows the strategy to speak to different ages while remaining relevant to digitally informed skincare shoppers.

# Competitor Positioning Map

## Two strategic dimensions

**X-axis:** Rational / science-led communication  
**Y-axis:** Experience / community-led communication

### Qualitative mapping

- Minimalist → high rational emphasis
- The Derma Co → high rational/concern emphasis
- Dot & Key → stronger experience emphasis
- Plum → stronger community/values emphasis

## Strategic whitespace

A potential whitespace is:

> **High trust + high decision simplicity**

This is intentionally not labeled as an empty market. Competitors may address parts of this territory. The opportunity is to make **routine decision support** the central organizing experience.

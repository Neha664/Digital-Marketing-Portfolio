# SWOT — Proposed Positioning

## Strengths
- Clear consumer problem
- Strong digital-content fit
- Reusable across search and social
- Supports education and conversion
- Creates a natural reason for first-party data collection

## Weaknesses
- Requires high-quality educational content
- Requires credible subject-matter review
- Routine recommendations need careful guardrails
- Differentiation must be maintained as competitors evolve

## Opportunities
- Routine builders
- AI-assisted discovery
- Search-led education
- Creator explainers
- Concern-based personalization
- CRM education
- Saveable social content

## Threats
- High category competition
- Similar ingredient messaging
- Promotional discounting
- Consumer skepticism
- Fast-moving social trends

# Market Gap Analysis

## Strategic Whitespace

### 1. Concern → Routine
Turn a concern into a clear sequence of actions.

### 2. Ingredient → Decision
Explain not only what an ingredient does, but when it makes sense.

### 3. Product → System
Move from individual product promotion toward routine systems.

### 4. Claim → Expectation
Explain realistic product roles and consistency.

### 5. Content → Tool
Convert education into interactive tools such as quizzes and routine builders.

## Opportunity statement

> **Own the decision layer between skincare information and skincare action.**

This is a strategic hypothesis derived from the market and competitor audit.

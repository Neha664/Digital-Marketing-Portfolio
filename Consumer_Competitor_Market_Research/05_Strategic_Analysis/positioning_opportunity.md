# Positioning Opportunity

## Strategic Territory

### **Skincare Without the Guesswork**

### Consumer tension
“I can find hundreds of skincare products. I still don't know what I actually need.”

### Brand role
Become the **decision-support layer** that simplifies skincare research into a practical routine.

### Value proposition

**Understand the concern.  
Learn the ingredient.  
Build the routine.  
Know what to expect.**

### Differentiation principle

Do not compete only on:
- more ingredients
- more products
- louder claims

Compete on:
- clarity
- guidance
- transparency
- consistency
- useful education

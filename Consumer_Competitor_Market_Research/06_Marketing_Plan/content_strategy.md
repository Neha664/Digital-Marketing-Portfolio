# Content Strategy

## Pillar 01 — Concern → Routine
Purpose: turn a problem into a practical path.

Formats:
- 30-second Reels
- carousel routines
- concern landing pages

## Pillar 02 — Ingredient → Explained
Purpose: build trust and search visibility.

Formats:
- ingredient cards
- comparison posts
- long-form articles

## Pillar 03 — Routine Mistakes
Purpose: high-save/share education.

Formats:
- “3 mistakes” Reels
- checklist carousels
- myth-vs-fact

## Pillar 04 — Expectation vs Reality
Purpose: reduce hype and strengthen credibility.

Formats:
- timeline explainers
- product-role content
- consistency education

## Pillar 05 — Interactive Discovery
Purpose: convert passive attention into first-party engagement.

Formats:
- skin-concern quiz
- routine finder
- story polls
- personalized email journey

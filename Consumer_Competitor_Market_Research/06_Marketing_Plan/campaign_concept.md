# Campaign Concept — #KnowYourRoutine

## Campaign idea

> **You don't need 10 products. You need the right routine.**

## Hero experience

A consumer selects a concern and receives:
1. a simple explanation
2. relevant ingredient education
3. a 3-step routine
4. usage/expectation guidance
5. relevant products
6. optional reminder journey

## Creative examples

### Reel
**Hook:** “Your skincare shelf is not a routine.”

### Carousel

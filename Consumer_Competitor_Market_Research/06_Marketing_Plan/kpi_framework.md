# KPI & Measurement Framework

## Funnel

### Awareness
Reach, impressions, video views, branded search lift.

### Engagement
Saves, shares, comments, completion rate, quiz participation.

### Consideration
Routine-builder starts, product-page visits, time on routine content, email/WhatsApp opt-ins.

### Conversion
Add-to-cart rate, conversion rate, revenue per visitor.

### Retention
Repeat purchase, re-engagement, cross-sell and CRM engagement.

## Experiment Design

### Hypothesis
Concern-led educational creative will create more qualified consideration than product-only creative.

### Control
Product-led creative.

### Test
Concern → ingredient → routine creative.

### Primary metric
Routine-builder completion/start rate.

### Secondary metrics
Product-page visits, add-to-cart rate and conversion rate.

> These are proposed KPIs and hypotheses, not observed campaign results.

# Customer Journey

| Stage | Consumer question | Brand job | Example asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | “Why am I facing this concern?” | Educate | Reel |
| Explore | “What ingredients matter?” | Explain | Carousel |
| Evaluate | “Which option fits me?” | Guide | Comparison |
| Decide | “What routine should I follow?” | Simplify | Routine builder |
| Purchase | “Is this credible?” | Prove | Evidence/reviews |
| Use | “Am I doing it correctly?” | Support | Email/WhatsApp |
| Return | “What should I add next?” | Personalize | Routine check-in |

## Strategic insight

The brand should not disappear after purchase. Education can become a **retention mechanism**, not only an acquisition tool.

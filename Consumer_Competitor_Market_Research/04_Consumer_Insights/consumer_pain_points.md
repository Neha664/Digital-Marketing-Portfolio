# Consumer Pain Points

## Pain Point 01 — Information overload
Too many products and ingredient claims can increase decision complexity.

## Pain Point 02 — Routine uncertainty
Consumers may know individual ingredients but not how to structure a routine.

## Pain Point 03 — Expectation mismatch
Benefit-led marketing can create uncertainty about realistic timelines and product roles.

## Pain Point 04 — Trust friction
Consumers increasingly encounter claims, reviews and advice from many sources.

## Pain Point 05 — Choice paralysis
A large catalog can make the “best” product harder to identify.

### Marketing response

The proposed strategy treats **decision clarity** as a consumer benefit.

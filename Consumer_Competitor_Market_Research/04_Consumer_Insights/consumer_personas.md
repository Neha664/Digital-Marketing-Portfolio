# Consumer Personas

## Persona 01 — Ingredient Researcher
**Job:** validate what an ingredient does before buying.

**Pain:** information is abundant but not always easy to interpret.

**Best content:** evidence summaries, ingredient comparisons, concentration explainers.

## Persona 02 — Routine Simplifier
**Job:** build an effective routine with fewer decisions.

**Pain:** product overload and uncertainty about order.

**Best content:** 3-step routines, AM/PM maps, routine builders.

## Persona 03 — Concern Solver
**Job:** solve one specific concern.

**Pain:** too many products claim to address the same concern.

**Best content:** concern pathways, decision trees, routine bundles.

## Persona 04 — Conscious Beauty Shopper
**Job:** balance efficacy with trust and values.

**Pain:** skepticism toward exaggerated claims.

**Best content:** formulation transparency, testing, realistic expectations and community proof.

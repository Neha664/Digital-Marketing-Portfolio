# Marketing Opportunities

## Opportunity 1 — Demand timing
Orders cluster around specific hours, enabling daypart-specific messaging.

## Opportunity 2 — Product merchandising
A small group of products contributes substantial volume/revenue, supporting hero-product placement.

## Opportunity 3 — Basket growth
Order-level basket size can support bundle and add-on experiments.

## Opportunity 4 — Better measurement
The current dataset cannot prove campaign performance. Adding campaign/channel/customer/offer fields would enable attribution and ROI analysis.

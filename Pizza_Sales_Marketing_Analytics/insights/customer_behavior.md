# Customer Behavior Insights

> The dataset does not contain customer IDs, so this document focuses on **order behavior**, not individual customer histories.

## Core signals
- Total orders: 21,350
- Average order value: $38.31
- Average pizzas per order: 2.32
- Highest order-volume day: Friday (3,538 orders)
- Strongest daypart by orders: Evening (7,911 orders)

## Marketing use
Use these signals to schedule communications, shape bundles, and design tests around demand patterns.

# Behavioral Segmentation Framework

Because the source data has no customer ID, the segmentation model uses **order-value behavior**, not individual customer identity.

### Entry
Smaller baskets. Test low-friction bundles and add-ons.

### Core
Typical order values. Use best-selling products and simple bundles.

### High-Value
Larger baskets. Test premium add-ons and cross-sell offers.

### Basket Builder
Very large baskets. Focus on bundle merchandising and group/family occasions.

These are hypotheses for marketing tests, not verified CRM segments.

# Promotional Strategy

## 1. Lunch bundles
Use strong lunch demand to test fixed-price meal combinations.

## 2. Evening bundles
Use evening demand for family/group bundles and add-on prompts.

## 3. Off-peak experiments
Use quieter periods for limited-time offers rather than discounting all day.

## 4. Product-led offers
Feature high-volume products in acquisition creative and high-revenue products in hero placements.

## 5. Measurement
Track offer code, channel, campaign ID, customer ID and incremental sales in future campaigns.
